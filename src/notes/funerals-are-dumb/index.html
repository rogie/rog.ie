I hate funerals in the US. they are fake and dumb. here's my wishlist for when i eat shit:

place:

back porch/fire (everyone's invited). i demand you stay til 2am. you can do it.

plot:

you all roll my dead ass off a cliff or push me into a body of water. no cremations. no burials.

officiant:

i shall nominate an irreverent friend, but i dont want that fake pastor shit. if you are nominated, you must embody the spirit with everything you have. otherwise, i will haunt your mortal ass.

food:

Spicy everything. i dont care if it hurts. just fucking do it. Here are your options: fried chicken sandos, smash burgers, thai food. this is all you have. choose.

music:

dubstep and hyperpop (I will attempt to give you playlists before I die). i'm updating my funeral playlist here.

invitees:

idgaf, whoever wants to have a fun time!

tone:

no fake "he was so awesome". my ultimate hope is to do things how i would do it. i desire deeply that people will connect as real humans, in ultimately vulnerability. share everything with eachother. share it all. don't be a fake ass *(#&$

activities:

hot sauce competition/dubstep mosh pit

grief:

losing an idiot is hard. losing an idiot named rogie might be like π times harder. we get it. life sucks. to be human is to lose. wtf?! yeah i hear you. but turns out, were like just mortal. weird. i get it.

grieving is best with others. take a drink. dive deep...seek to share stories of your loved one with those that knew them. one of my favorite memories grieving was about my gram, sharing whiskey on the back porch, talking about her life, and how she was as a person. i truly think this is the esseence of a funeral: to carry their legacy in your conversations. if my gram was in the room, what would she think?
